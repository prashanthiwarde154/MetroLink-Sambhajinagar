Facilities for Women Passengers

  • Half coach at both the end of train is reserved for women.
  • Signage have been provided at platform to indicate where the reserved coach normally stops.
  • Reserved seats in every coach.
  • For Checking male passengers travelling in women's coach
    • Quick reaction teams have been deployed.
    • Special drives are carried out
    • Offenders will be penalized for 500/-
  • Zero tolerance for drunkards and trouble makers.
  • CCTV surveillance in MMRC stations premises /Metro trains.
  • Patrolling of the platform after sunset hours have been intensified.
  • Female CISF staff has been deployed at stations for frisking.

Facilities for Differently-abled Person

  • Extra Wide Automatic flap gates for wheelchairs
  • Provision of wheelchair at stations
  • Reserved spaces for wheelchair in trains
  • Tactile paths for visually impaired
  • Separate toilets
  • Ramps for change in the level
  • Handrails alongside stairs
  • Lifts are provided with
    • Wide access doors
    • Hand rails
    • Audio-visual indications
    • Telephone buttons
    • Call buttons in braille
  • Signage at prominent locations for guidance
  • Seats reserved in trains
  • Audio/visual indication while closing of train doors
  • Station staff provides personal assistance
