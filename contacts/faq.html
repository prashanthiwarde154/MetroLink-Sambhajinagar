Frequently Asked Questions

How long is MML-3 (Phase 1)? How many stations are there? +

MML-3 Phase 1 is approximately 33.5 km long and consists of 27 stations across the city.

Who will operate the MML-3? +

The metro will be operated by Metro Link Sambhajinagar Rail Corporation under government supervision.

What is the headway between two trains? +

The average headway between two trains will be 3 to 5 minutes during peak hours.

Are all stations accessible? +

Yes, all metro stations are designed with lifts, escalators, and ramps for accessibility.

Is there any size/weight limit for luggage? +

Yes. Passengers are allowed to carry luggage up to 15 kg with maximum dimensions not exceeding 80 cm x 50 cm x 30 cm. Oversized or excessively heavy luggage is not permitted to ensure passenger safety and comfort.

Is there any list of prohibited materials at Metro Stations? +

The following items are strictly prohibited inside metro premises:

  • Explosives and firecrackers
  • Firearms and sharp weapons
  • Flammable liquids and gases
  • Toxic and hazardous chemicals
  • Any item that may cause harm or panic

Security personnel may inspect baggage at entry points.

Can passengers bring eatables inside the metro trains? +

Passengers may carry packed food items. However, eating inside metro trains and station premises is strictly prohibited to maintain cleanliness and hygiene.

Are inflammable items, e.g., kerosene, petrol, etc., allowed in metro trains? +

No. Inflammable substances such as kerosene, petrol, diesel, LPG cylinders, and similar hazardous materials are strictly prohibited inside metro stations and trains for safety reasons.

Are pets allowed in metro trains? +

Pets and animals are not allowed inside metro trains or station premises, except certified service animals assisting persons with disabilities.

Is liquor allowed in metro trains? +

No. Carrying or consuming liquor inside metro trains and stations is strictly prohibited.

Is raw meat and fish allowed in metro trains? +

Raw meat and fish are not allowed inside metro trains due to hygiene and odor concerns. Properly sealed packaged food items may be permitted subject to security inspection.

Can I purchase a ticket online? +

Yes. Passengers can purchase tickets online through the official Metro Link Sambhajinagar website or mobile application. After booking, a QR code ticket will be issued which can be scanned at the AFC gates.

Can I extend my journey beyond my ticket limit? +

Yes. If you travel beyond your ticketed destination, you must pay the fare difference at the destination station’s Customer Care Center before exiting the paid area.

What is the time limit for a single journey ticket & return journey ticket? +

A single journey ticket is valid for one continuous trip and must be used within 75 minutes from entry. A return journey ticket is valid for the same day and must be used within the operational hours of the metro service.

What types of tickets are issued at the station? +

The following ticket types are available:

  • Single Journey Ticket (Token/QR)
  • Return Journey Ticket
  • Smart Card (Rechargeable)
Is there any facility for a refund if I miss the last train? +

Refund requests may be considered at the Customer Care Center if the last train is missed due to operational reasons. Valid proof of ticket must be provided.

Where can I purchase single and return journey tickets? +

Passengers can purchase tickets from:

  • Ticket Vending Machines (TVM)
  • Customer Care Counters at metro stations
  • Official website or mobile app
What should you do if you lose your ticket? +

If a ticket is lost, passengers must immediately report to the Customer Care Center. A penalty along with the maximum applicable fare may be charged to exit the station.

What kinds of currencies does a TVM accept? +

Ticket Vending Machines accept Indian currency notes and coins. Selected machines may also support UPI, debit cards, and digital payments.

How to issue a ticket from TVM? +

To issue a ticket from TVM:

  • Select destination station
  • Select number of passengers
  • Make payment using available options
  • Collect printed QR ticket or token
What are the criteria for children's tickets? +

Children below 3 feet (90 cm) in height may travel free when accompanied by an adult. Children above this height require a valid ticket.

What are the safety measures available at metro stations? +

Metro Link Sambhajinagar stations are equipped with advanced safety systems including:

  • 24/7 CCTV surveillance
  • Security personnel at all entry and exit points
  • Baggage screening machines
  • Fire detection and suppression systems
  • Emergency alarms and help points

Regular safety drills are also conducted to ensure passenger protection.

What are the steps taken for women safety? +

For women safety, the metro provides:

  • Dedicated security staff including female personnel
  • Continuous CCTV monitoring
  • Emergency help buttons inside trains
  • Well-lit platforms and station premises

Passengers can immediately contact station staff or use emergency intercom systems if needed.

What if a passenger falls on the track when there are no trains? +

If a passenger falls onto the track, nearby passengers should immediately inform station staff or press the emergency alarm. Trained personnel will assist in safely rescuing the individual while ensuring train movement is halted.

What happens when the platform gets overcrowded? +

During overcrowding, station staff manage passenger flow by regulating entry, making public announcements, and deploying additional security personnel to maintain order and safety.

What happens if a metro train stops between stations? +

If a train stops between stations due to technical or operational reasons, announcements will be made inside the train. The issue will be resolved promptly by the control center. Passengers are advised to remain calm and not attempt to open doors or exit the train unless instructed by staff.

Who has the authority to certify the safety of the project? +

The metro project is certified for safety by the authorized government body such as the Commissioner of Metro Railway Safety (CMRS) before public operations begin.
